# Dub Techno — sources and what was taken from them

The research was done 2026-11-14/15 for `techno/16-minimal-dub`, then for
`techno/17-halfstep-dub` / `-v2`. Raw session log:
`docs/thoughts/dub_session.md`.

## External guides

### Tunary — How to make dub techno
https://tunaryinstruments.com/guides/how-to-make-dub-techno/

What was taken:
- 120–130 BPM; the core is 120–125.
- Muffled four-on-the-floor, soft offbeat hats.
- A short filtered chord stab through a long delay/reverb.
- Deep warm sub, hiss/fog, patient arrangement.
- Small changes carry the track; new sections aren't needed.

### Studio Brootle — Dub Techno Tutorial: 4 Ingredients
https://www.studiobrootle.com/dub-techno-tutorial-4-ingredients/

What was taken:
- Four ingredients: kick, sub, hats, 2 chords.
- Everything else is filters, delay/reverb and automation.
- Delay/reverb are not decoration but the sound itself.
- Space and restraint matter more than the number of events.

### House of Loop — How to create the iconic dub techno chord sound
https://houseofloop.com/how-to-create-the-iconic-dub-techno-chord-sound/

What was taken:
- Stab through a ping-pong delay with feedback around 40–65 %.
- Repeats darker than the dry signal: filter every echo.
- Reverb after the delay, not instead of it.
- Minimal hypnotic 4/4, tape warmth, long tails.

## Scene / what to listen to

These are not "sources with URLs" but a canon for ear training. Listen not to
melodies, but to **space, filters, delays and the amount of emptiness**.

- **Basic Channel** — minimum events, noise/hiss, the chord lives in the echo,
  the top is rolled off, the low end is the center.
- **Rhythm & Sound** — dub logic, sub, vocal fragments, endless
  delays; not reggae stylization, but space.
- **Maurizio** — dry minimal loops, the stab as a rhythmic element.
- **Chain Reaction** — noise, hiss, long forms, almost no events.
- **Deepchord / Echospace / cv313** — field, reverb, underwater low end,
  very slow timbral shifts.
- **Monolake** — clicks, sub, minimal electronics; useful for the
  STRIP/CLICK modes.
- **Pole / Vladislav Delay** — glitch dub, cracks, space.
- **Fluxion / Porter Ricks / Deadbeat / Yagya / Substance / Vainqueur /
  Scion** — different shades: from dark ambient dub to minimal 4/4.
- **Burial** — not dub techno, but the source of the halfstep rhythm, vocal pad
  and "rainy" space for `17-halfstep-dub`.

## Strudel

- https://strudel.cc/learn/ — table of contents.
- https://strudel.cc/learn/mini-notation/ — `~`, `[]`, `<>`, `*`, `,`,
  `:`; all the phrases are built from these.
- https://strudel.cc/learn/synths/ — `sawtooth`, `triangle`, `sine`,
  `unison`, `detune`, `partials`, FM.
- https://strudel.cc/learn/effects/ — `lpf`, `lpq`, `lpdepth`, `lpsync`,
  `delay`, `delaytime`, `delayfeedback`, `room`, `roomsize`, `chorus`.
- https://club.tidalcycles.org — Tidal Club; most techniques
  carry over to Strudel.
- https://codeberg.org/uzu/strudel — sources and issues.

## Samples and maps

- `github:tidalcycles/uzu-drumkit` — `bd`, `hh`, `oh`, `rim`, `sh`,
  `sd`, `cp` and so on. Good for a muffled kick, soft hats, rim.
- `github:tidalcycles/Dirt-Samples` — `click`, `wind`, `noise2`,
  `space`, `birds`, `insects`, `padlong` and so on.
- `crackle` and `pink` — synthesized textures, not samples.
- The PRELOAD SAMPLES button in the panel downloads only the keys in use;
  for `16-minimal-dub` that's 45 files.

## What not to take

- EDM transitions: risers, white-noise builds, drops, sidechain pumping,
  snare rolls.
- A "pretty" lead and a melody on top of the chords.
- 303 chord licks "pew" on every note.
- Reggae chords on every weak beat, if reggae wasn't asked for.
- Bright top end and over-compression.
