### #4 · darenager · 2022-05-29

Not quite sure what you exactly want to do either, but some ideas:

Chop the breakbeat using slices, lay out the trigs to get it playing how you want, change amp settings to shorten sounds, slow the attack etc.

Use the lofi effect, you can reduce the sample rate, bit depth, distort and modulate the amplitude which can go from fades to tremolo to a kind of ring mod.

Filter, can distort or cut bits out of the spectrum, you can with high resonance get it to ping/ring too.

Comb filter, you can add harmonics or make it metallic sounding etc.

Try the other fx too, and go nuts with resampling, plocks, scenes etc.
