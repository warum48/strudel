So I made this track.

What I did was I took the drum stem from another breakcore track that made and manually chopped it up to match up with a ghost kick snare pattern. And automated gross beat as an fx to glitch the drum pattern

The bass I messed around with the pitch envelope in sytrus the tunings, used oscillators as lfos modulated by other oscillators..used volume and filter envelopes..slight sidechain to the ghost drum…did two patches or three via using one synth…combined the dry signal and the fx treated signal…automated the volume the filters and gross beat to get glitch bass.

Admittedly the leads and the melodies could be worked on more because the sounds don’t exactly compliment each other.

But it still somewhat works musically.
