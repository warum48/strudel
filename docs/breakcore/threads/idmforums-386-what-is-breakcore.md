### #51 · Mecha_Twitchy · 2024-08-21

Hey! I make that…

Breakcore is a type of Hardcore Techno genre, most people hear an Amen Break at >=160 bpm and go “uuhhh breakcore brrrrrrr,” but breakcore’s specifically about the BREAKBEAT. I know that a lot of modern breakcore (what’s ACTUALLY breakcore) is a mess of different breakbeats with a time signature FAR beyond the lump of the IQ curve, but it’s not always crazy shit with the snare and kick beats in switched places. Breakcore evolved from genres like DnB, so the break carried through, but Breakcore deformed the drum rhythm to extreme complexities and used other sound samples in place of actual instruments. Another thing that kept on accelerating from DnB was the tempo, which gives Breakcore it’s energy.

Go listen to some Machine Girl.
