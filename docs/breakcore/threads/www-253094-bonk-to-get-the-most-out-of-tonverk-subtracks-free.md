So after about six months with Tonverk I started to get frustrated with how deep the potential of Subtracks are for drum machine duties, versus how limited they are to set up on device. I mean, TV has an autosampler for .elmultis, and theres LoopSeek for going deeper, but there’s nothing for Subtracks that lets you customize velocity layers, round robins, etc. Most people have had to write scripts or even hand-edit .eldrum files.

Soooo I built a free+ tool that helps you get the most out of TV’s Subtracks. You can build complex, deep kits fast, from files (free) or even sample direct from hardware or plugins (paid). It’s actually even… fun? (Well, I tried to make it as fun as possible. You be the judge!) Check it out here:

Save and load templates

BONK 1.3.0 is live (http://downloads.bonkthedrums.com/BONK.dmg) :drum:

Thanks for the hardware-sampling feedback this week. This release makes the Pro hardware-sampling a bit tighter and gives you control over the audio settings. I have not had a chance to test it with my TV and wont until at least the weekend, so please let me know if it does/n’t work.

New

Sample-rate + buffer-size controls in the Hardware tab. Set your capture rate (Auto / 44.1k / 48k / 96k) and buffer size (256 / 512 / 1024 / 2048). “Auto” detects your device’s native rate.

Fixed

USB-audio crackles during hardware sampling. BONK now captures at your device’s native sample rate and resamples cleanly to 48k, instead of forcing 48k and letting Core Audio do a lossy conversion. This was the likely cause of the T-8 / TX-6 crackling.

Hardware tab silencing playback. Switching to the Hardware tab no longer kills sample preview; no more toggling the output device to get sound back.

The in-app update banner should prompt you, or grab it from the site (http://downloads.bonkthedrums.com/BONK.dmg).
