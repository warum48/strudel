# 07 · Anti-patterns (EN)

Every item comes with a quote and a fix.

---

## 1. Over-chopping

**Symptom:** the track turns into "spastic noise" with no musical meaning.

> "I end up going overboard … it becomes more just spastic noise rather than music"
> `[44354#1]`.

> "You loose all the groove if you chop too tight" `[226901]`.

**Fix:**
- Cut on 1/4 and 1/8, not 16ths `[226901]`.
- Go back to 3–5 markers `[175570]`.
- Keep one layer completely unchopped (the full loop) as an anchor.

---

## 2. Removed note-offs

**Symptom:** the break sounds "muddy and muffled"; at high tempo it turns to mush.

> "removing them makes the sound muddy and muffled" `[18985#46]`.

**Fix:** put the note-off back before every next sample `[29927#1]`; alternatively a
downward `07xx` or a short amp-env.

---

## 3. Warping / time-stretching chops

**Symptom:** the groove disappears, everything sounds "even and dead".

> "Warp is the groove killer" `[175570]`.

**Fix:** re-pitch instead of warping; warp only kick/snare, leave ghost notes alone
`[175570]`; `×1.5` tempo = `+7` semitones `[226901]`.

---

## 4. Chasing resolution

**Symptom:** 800 BPM / 256 LPB, but the track did not improve.

> "pushing your speed to a point where it doesn't really matter anymore … it's just
> going to be like an ocd kind of thing" `[18985#65]`.

**Fix:** 8–16 LPB is enough; 8 LPB @ 400 BPM = 16 LPB @ 200 BPM `[29927#21]`.

---

## 5. Pattern theatre

**Symptom:** 18 patterns, but all three variations.

> "18 drum patterns firing off here, they are all really similar, built on just 3 basic
> variations" `[6643#15]`.

**Fix:** a 4-bar cadence with real mutation `[44354#6]`; count ideas, not patterns.

---

## 6. The randomizer as author

**Symptom:** everything is "random", nothing is memorable.

> "stay way from randomizers !!!" `[173927]`.
> "Nothing should be random. That's the 'Intelligent' part of IDM" `[173927]`.

**Fix:** use "2 of 4" conditions instead of percentages `[173927]`; chance only on
fills (50–60 %, then ramp) `[173927]`; randomize parameters (decay, sample start),
not notes `[173927]`.

---

## 7. Too many layers / two 16th figures

**Symptom:** "muddy chaos", nothing reads.

> "if u got one 16th sequence running prominent already, the last thing u need yet, is
> another 16th sequence on top of it" `[173927]`.

**Fix:** ≤3 elements, one per frequency floor `[173927]`; remove a layer, do not add.

---

## 8. Too much sustain

**Symptom:** everything smears, no attack.

> "Too much sustain and looseness will result in a mess quickly. Keep it tight"
> `[173927]`.

**Fix:** staccato by default `[173927]`; short amp-env; note-off.

---

## 9. Grit instead of attack

**Symptom:** distortion ate the transient, the hit does not read; melody no longer
sits on top.

> "its always a problem for me to add melody on top" `[17702#5]`.

**Fix:** distortion after the filter `[002-jungle]`; bit-crush only individual hits
`[173927]`; keep a clean layer for contrast `[17702#3]`.

---

## 10. Copying an artist

**Symptom:** the track is a pastiche of Venetian Snares/Aphex with nothing of its own.

> "don't try to be like Venetian Snares. Find your own thing" `[21030#10]`.
> "nobody - including me - really enjoys a copy-cat" `[29927#13]`.

**Fix:** copy the **technique**, not the track; "every minute spent 'wondering' how
someone does it is a minute wasted" `[34022#18]`; the exercise is to copy one track
beat for beat and then forget it `[34022#18]`.

---

## 11. Micro-editing perfectionism

**Symptom:** endless tweaking, the track never ships.

> "every time I do I just go down a micro editing spiral without getting anything done"
> `[2328#20]`.

**Fix:** timebox the break (BotB: 2 hours on drums `[18985#46]`); finish the skeleton
first, decorate later.

---

## 12. Unsynced break switching

**Symptom:** swapping the break makes hits "move", layering conflicts.

> "hits have moved slightly, due to the played groove … clashes or beats that sound out
> of time" `[34022#29]`.

**Fix:** sync without warping `[29927#18]`; rebuild the layering for the new break;
3–4 breaks per track maximum `[18985#66]`.

---

## 13. No contrast

**Symptom:** maximum density the whole time, nothing memorable.

**Fix:** a "plain" passage before the return `[6643#7]`; silence as an element
`[173927]`; density change matters more than note change `[03-arrangement]`.

---

## 14. Genre gatekeeping instead of work

**Symptom:** arguing "is this breakcore" instead of making a track.

> "breakcore is a rather abstract genre that doesn't NECESSARILY require melody, rythm
> or soundquality" `[18985#67]`.

**Fix:** one criterion — the drums lead and the break is audible. Everything else is
taste.

---

## Quick diagnostic questionnaire

1. Can you tell the kick from the snare? → if not, too many layers/distortion.
2. Are there gaps between hits? → if not, note-off.
3. Does the groove read at low volume? → if not, too dense.
4. Is there a section without chopping? → if not, add an anchor.
5. Does something change every 4 bars? → if not, mutate.
6. Would you recognize the track a week later? → if not, remove a layer and simplify.
