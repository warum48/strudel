## What the track uses

- 5 complex-oscillator pairs: WT + FM, LFO scanning, FM index.
- Waveshaping/shape and warp, not just a filter.
- Ducking as AM-like pair coupling.
- Drone and dirt as support/instability.
- `mode` clean/grit/fold/scream.

## What is not here

- Audio-rate cross-layer FM for wavetables.
- Vactrol/LPG non-linearity.
- A full Buchla patch matrix.
- Presets or emulation of a specific module.
