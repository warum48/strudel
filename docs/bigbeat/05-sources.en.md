# Big Beat — sources

Read for this digest (2026-11-24). Each entry says what it actually confirmed;
this is an index, not a prose summary of each page.

## Genre definition and history

- **Wikipedia — Big beat**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_beat
  Heavy distorted breakbeats, 100–140 BPM, TB-303 lines, 60s/70s funk/soul/
  jazz/rock loops, pop/house/techno song structures, crescendos/builds/drops,
  extended rolls, spoken word/film/siren/explosion effects, filters/phasing/
  flanging. 1989 term (Iain Williams, Big Bang), Big Beat Boutique 1995–2001,
  peak 1995–1999, decline from 2001, chart numbers, Matrix/Wipeout placement.
- **AllMusic — Big Beat genre**: https://www.allmusic.com/genre/big-beat-ma0000004997
  Scene labels (Skint, Wall of Sound, City of Angels), sample-based party
  music, chemical brothers/prodigy as partial fits, criticism of gimmickry.
- **Vice — In Defense of Big Beat**: https://www.vice.com/en/article/big-beat-history-influence-prodigy-fatboy-slim/
  Mid-tempo 90–120, plunderphonics formula in six steps, labels/club, role in
  exporting electronic music to the US.
- **EDM Bible — Big beat**: https://edmbible.com/glossary/big-beat/
  100–140 BPM, UK mid-90s, heavy looped breaks, party/crossover focus.
- **What The Genre — Big beat**: https://www.whatthegenre.com/map/station/big-beat
  Concise definition: heavy breaks + TB-303 + 60s/70s samples.
- **Classic Pop — Popscene: Big Beat**: https://www.classicpopmag.com/features/popscene-big-beat/
  Propellerheads/*Decksandrumsandrockandroll*, Mercury nomination, *Matrix* /
  *Tomorrow Never Dies* / first iPod commercial placement.

## Tutorials and technique (read)

- **MusicRadar — Beat Building: how to make a Big Beat beat**:
  https://www.musicradar.com/news/beat-building-how-to-build-a-big-beat-beat
  Sample selection above all; James Gang "Ashes, The Rain And I" → "Right Here,
  Right Now"; slice the break in a sampler (poly), don't convert to MIDI; 135
  BPM; keep most of the 2-bar pattern + extra kicks; 909 kit over it; synth
  kick for a heavy "1"; tape sim/compressor on the master; acid bass with
  automation; free SampleRadar breaks.
- **MusicRadar — How to get the classic breakbeat sound**:
  https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/classic-breakbeat-sound
  Crop/warp, slice by transients, trigger from C1, resequence notes, per-slice
  compressor/delay/distortion, extract groove vs quantise, bounce and retrigger
  for stutters/chops.
- **MusicRadar — How to layer monstrously phat breakbeats**:
  https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-layer-monstrously-phat-breakbeats-210120
  Don't put layers on the same frequency; analyser on the kick (~50 Hz); pitch
  controls; break loop on top; bus processing to gel.
- **MusicRadar — How to build a layered '90s-style rave breakbeat**:
  https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-build-a-layered-90s-style-rave-breakbeat-636596
  Two ~120 BPM breaks played at 150, bitcrusher/DAC resampling, bass boost +
  snare layer, bounce then chop, 808 kick 4/4 for sub.
- **MusicRadar — How to build a drum 'n' bass breakbeat**:
  https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-build-a-drum-n-bass-breakbeat-621105
  Same break-making chain: multiband comp/saturation on kick, distorted snare
  top, narrow room, vinyl-noise bed under the bus, render and re-pitch.
- **Attack Magazine — Big Beat Techno (Beat Dissected)**:
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/big-beat-techno/
  130 BPM, 50% swing, woody kick pushed low (Boom/Drive/Glue, −6 st), late
  16th off-beat kicks as fake sidechain, short low tom, rim on 2/4, off-beat
  hat, glue compressor + echo.
- **Attack Magazine — Mixing Breakbeats**:
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/mixing-breakbeats/
  Snare/hats/shakers must be felt; top-end shine comes from them.

## Examples and scene lists (read)

- **NPR/KEXP — The Big Beat Revolution: 11 Essential Songs**:
  https://www.npr.org/2011/08/19/139754112/the-big-beat-revolution-11-essential-songs
  The 11 scene picks with the picker's rationale (Howlett, Cook, Harris,
  White, Gifford, Carter, etc.); "Chemical Beats" as originator, "Brothers
  Gonna Work It Out" as blueprint, "Private Psychedelic Reel" as peak.
- **Mixmag — The 10 best big beat tracks released pre-'98**:
  https://mixmag.net/feature/the-10-best-big-beat-tracks-released-pre-98
  "Breathe", "Block Rockin' Beats", "Busy Child", "Spybreak!", "Everybody
  Needs A 303", "Psychopath", "Vanilla", "Kool Rok Bass", sampled riffs.
- **RTÉ — Top 100 90s Dance Anthems**:
  https://www.rte.ie/radio/gold/stories/1477879-top-100-90s-dance-anthems/
  Rankings for "Hey Boy Hey Girl" (#4), "Firestarter" (#6), "Praise You" (#9),
  "Block Rockin' Beats" (#32), "Rockafeller Skank" (#42).
- **AllMusic — Big Beat Song Highlights**:
  https://www.allmusic.com/style/big-beat-ma0000004997/songs
  "Setting Sun", "Life Is Sweet", "Praise You", "Breathe", "Song to the
  Siren", "We Have Explosive", "Block Rockin' Beats", "Come With Us".
- **Sonaa — Big Beat: history, key tracks and production guide**:
  https://sonaa.ca/en/styles/breaks/big-beat/
  120–140 BPM, 16 reference tracks, listening notes for the four archetypes
  (compressed break, stuttered vocal, punk break, elegant spy break).

## Note-level material (added 2026-11-24, used by `06-practice.en.md`)

- **Mind Flux / Michael L Penman — Sample Hacker: Block Rockin' Beats**:
  https://www.mind-flux.com/news-1/2023/1/27/sample-hacker-block-rockin-beats
  109.5 BPM tap; The Crusaders bass notes E1 → A#1 → C2, final note +3 st;
  Purdie "Changes" break = 3 bars + 3 sixteenths, dotted-8th segment, fill in
  last 3 beats of bar 4, open hat on last 16th of beat 2 from bar 5, vocal stab
  on step 1 for 3 bars then rest.
- **Ableton 12 Sound Design Atlas — Big Beat Track**:
  https://ableton.onefootstep.us/deconstructions/big-beat-track.html
  Archetype at 132 BPM, E minor blues (E G A Bb B D), bass E0/E1 vs acid E2–E3,
  16 slices over 2 bars (227 ms each), build HP 30→2k / Redux 44→8k / roll
  vel 70→127 / ghost kicks on last two 16ths of each beat, rolling breakdown
  (kick step 1, snare step 9), false ending. Claims labelled documented/by ear.
- **Ableton 12 Sound Design Atlas — Famous Big Beat & Breakbeat Records**:
  https://ableton.onefootstep.us/deconstructions/famous-big-beat-breakbeat-tracks.html
  "Chemical Beats" = Juno-106 through Boss Heavy Metal pedal (documented);
  "Praise You" = two S950 kits + SE-1 bass + live 303 (documented).
- **Attack Magazine — Big Beat Techno**:
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/big-beat-techno/
  130 BPM, 50% swing, kick on 1/5/9/13, quiet extra kicks on last two 16ths of
  each beat, rim on 1/5/9/13 + 2nd 16th of beats 2/4, offbeat hat, low tom on
  2nd 16th of beat 4.
- **theprodigy.info / dbanz — Firestarter guitar tabs**:
  https://theprodigy.info/tabs/firestarter.html ,
